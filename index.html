FaB Tracker Privacy Policy

Developer: Juan Becker

Effective date: October 23, 2026

FaB Tracker is a companion app for the Flesh and Blood trading card game. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app.

Summary

  • No accounts. You never sign up or log in.
  • Your data stays on your device.
  • No tracking, analytics, or advertising.
  • We do not sell or share your data.

Data stored on your device

Your matches, events, decks, life-counter history, card collection, and statistics are stored locally on your device using Apple's SwiftData. App preferences (such as haptics) are stored in the device's standard settings storage. This data is not uploaded to us or anyone else. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Network requests

To show public game information, the app makes read-only requests to:

  • The app's backend server (hosted at vibecode.run) to fetch public card data and Living Legend leaderboard standings. When you search for cards or browse the card catalog, your search text and filters are sent with the request so the server can return matching results. No personal information, account identifier, device identifier, or your locally stored data is included.
  • Public image hosts (Legend Story Studios' Amazon S3/CloudFront storage and Google Cloud Storage) to load card and hero artwork.

Like any internet request, these servers may receive your IP address and basic technical request details (for example, in standard server logs). We do not use this information to identify you or track you.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect names, email addresses, contacts, location, photos, or other personal data.
  • We do not use analytics, advertising, or third-party tracking SDKs.
  • We do not track you across apps or websites.
  • We do not sell or share your data with third parties.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this location with a new effective date.
